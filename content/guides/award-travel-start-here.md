## Define the trip first
Start with your origin, destination, date flexibility, number of travelers, preferred cabin, and acceptable positioning airports.

## Search before you transfer
Flexible bank points can be valuable because you have options. Transfers to airline or hotel programs are commonly one-way, so confirm bookable space and the final price before moving points whenever possible.

## Check multiple paths
Search nonstop flights, one-stop itineraries, nearby airports, and separate positioning flights. Make sure separate tickets leave enough buffer for delays and baggage re-checks.

## Compare the full cost
An award is not free if it carries large taxes, surcharges, positioning costs, or a hotel night you would not otherwise need.

## Keep a backup
Schedule changes happen. Save alternative flights and understand the program's change and cancellation rules before your trip.
