## Before applying
- Confirm you can pay the statement balance in full.
- Compare the public offer and referral offer.
- Read the issuer's eligibility language.
- Know the annual fee and the benefits you realistically expect to use.
- Consider how a new account fits your broader credit goals.

## After approval
- Add the card to your tracker.
- Record the open date and annual-fee date.
- Save the application offer terms.
- Add any monthly, quarterly, or annual credits to your reminders.

## Before renewal
- Total the benefits you actually used.
- Separate points value from cash credits.
- Decide whether you would keep the card without a referral bonus or welcome offer.
- Review product-change or cancellation implications before taking action.
