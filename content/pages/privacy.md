Reynolds Restful Getaways does not intentionally collect sensitive financial information. The site may process basic usage information and information you voluntarily submit, such as an email address when requesting travel notes.

## Newsletter requests
When you submit your email address through the newsletter form, the address and basic form metadata are processed by FormSubmit so the signup request can be delivered to Reynolds Restful Getaways. FormSubmit may apply its own spam-prevention and processing practices under its privacy terms.

The site also stores a small browser-only flag after a successful signup so the same browser shows the request as completed instead of repeatedly submitting the form. This flag stays on that device/browser and does not contain your email address.

If newsletter emails are sent, each message should include a reasonable way to unsubscribe. You can also contact `hello@reynoldsrestfulgetaways.com` to request removal from the list.

## Analytics
If analytics are enabled in the future, this section will be updated to identify the provider and the information it processes.

## External links
Links to banks, airlines, hotels, affiliate partners, and other third parties are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
