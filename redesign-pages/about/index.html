From Paris to Tokyo, and the plans in between

Making the world

Paris showed me what points and miles could make possible. Tokyo proved it wasn’t a one-time thing. Learning how rewards programs work changed the way my wife and I approached travel.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in Finance at the University of the Cumberlands and my associate’s degree in Information Technology at Elizabethtown Community & Technical College. I now work as a financial analyst in healthcare.

That analytical background shapes how I plan: compare the options, understand the costs, and choose what fits. Here, I pair that mindset with lessons from our own trips to make points and miles feel approachable.
